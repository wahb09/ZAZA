## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an haute-couture digital exhibition and immersive luxury narrative experience. Created as an intimate, emotional testament, the design language rejects commercialized, saccharine romantic clichés in favor of nocturnal elegance, celestial depth, and high-editorial gravity. 

The aesthetic is **Atmospheric Digital Luxury** interwoven with **Nocturnal Glassmorphism**:
- Visual motifs favor cosmic intimacy—ethereal star-point particles, deep chiaroscuro contrasts, gossamer light caustics, and restrained metaphysical typography over decorative novelties.
- The interface acts as a cinematic frame, prioritizing silent space, deliberate pacing, and museum-grade typographic precision.
- Every state conveys tactility through subtle luminous refractions, translucent surfaces, and micro-interactions that glide rather than snap.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in shadowy depths, accented with warm radiance and restrained precious metals:

- **Canvases & Substrates:** 
  - Primary Background: Deep Void Cherry (`#0E050A`).
  - Elevated Container / Secondary Void: Nocturnal Black Cherry (`#16090F`).
  - Structural Layer / Mid-Ground: Midnight Burgundy (`#2A0A19`) transitioning to Deep Wine (`#3B1024`).
- **Chromatic Accents:**
  - **Romantic Crimson (`#E83F67`):** Primary interactive focal points, active states, and emotional climaxes. Never applied as flat flood fills; always paired with deep base shadows or radial luminescence.
  - **Champagne Gold (`#D9B477`):** Secondary accent reserved for precious metadata, delicate borders, timestamps, and monogram details.
  - **Radiant Blush (`#FFB5C8`):** Tertiary highlight used for celestial particle glow, soft internal card gradients, and ethereal glow reflections.
- **Typographic & Illuminative Neutrals:**
  - Primary Text & Highlights: Illuminated Warm Ivory (`#FFF3EA`).
  - Muted Editorial Text: Warm Ash Rose (`#B89F9E`).
  - Whispered Metadata & Disabled States: Deep Smoked Mauve (`#644E57`).
  - Border Glass Hairlines: 10–18% alpha overlays of Champagne Gold (`rgba(217, 180, 119, 0.14)`) or Radiant Blush (`rgba(255, 181, 200, 0.12)`).

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances poetic grandeur with architectural discipline:

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes the lineage of bespoke luxury publishing and classical romantic inscriptions. Italic variants must be used selectively for narrative cadence, proper nouns, and intimate asides.
- **Interface & Narrative Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its clean, geometric clarity and open apertures. Set with generous line heights to provide optical rest amid atmospheric backdrops.
- **Labeling & Metadata:** Micro-labels and navigational cues are set in uppercase with wide tracking (`0.18em`) to mirror luxury curation standards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a responsive 12-column dynamic framework designed to echo cinema aspect ratios:

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, max-width `1440px`, centered with generous gutters (`2.5rem`) and expansive margins (`5rem`). White space (negative space) functions as an intentional storytelling void.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid with fluid scaling, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` side padding.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid system with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Dynamic vertical spacing contracts to preserve visual continuity while retaining breathing room around display headings.
- **Rhythm:** Layout compositions alternate between full-bleed cinematic viewing windows and concentrated text columns (max 680px) to enhance readability and poetic focus.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is generated through optical luminosity and dark atmospheric layering rather than physical drop shadows:

- **Base Void (Level 0):** Pure `#0E050A` canvas carrying slow-drifting, semi-opaque starlight dust and dark burgundy radial glows.
- **Floating Glass Surfaces (Level 1):** Midnight Burgundy tinted fills (`rgba(42, 10, 25, 0.55)`) backed by a high-intensity backdrop blur (`24px` to `32px`). Wrapped in a crisp `1px` translucent champagne/blush hairline border (`rgba(217, 180, 119, 0.15)`).
- **Interactive Planes & Modals (Level 2):** Layered wine acrylic (`rgba(59, 16, 36, 0.75)`) with `40px` blur, bordered by `1px` blush sheen (`rgba(255, 181, 200, 0.25)`), anchored by an ambient celestial aura: `0 20px 60px -10px rgba(232, 63, 103, 0.18)`.
- **Specular Accents:** Glowing micro-radii on hover states, emitting a soft localized aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 25px rgba(232, 63, 103, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The design uses **Soft Geometric** contours (`roundedness: 1`), embodying modern luxury:

- Architectural precision dominates: primary interactive containers, panels, and cards employ subtle, tailored edge radii (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`).
- Controlled pill geometry (`9999px`) is reserved exclusively for interactive capsule buttons, refined meta-tags, and bespoke media playback indicators.
- Pure sharp cuts (`0px`) are applied strictly to horizontal and vertical architectural divider lines and narrative dividers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Celestial Action:** High-gloss capsule or soft-curved block. Base layer transitions from Romantic Crimson (`#E83F67`) into deep wine gradient; illuminated ivory typography (`#FFF3EA`). Hover state expands a gentle blush corona aura (`rgba(232, 63, 103, 0.3)`) with a `0.2s` ease.
- **Secondary Champagne Stroke:** Semitransparent dark cherry surface framed with a 1px Champagne Gold border (`#D9B477`). Text set in ivory with subtle tracked uppercase caps.
- **Ghost Narrative Trigger:** Unbordered inline trigger in ivory with a hairline gold underline that glides outward from center upon pointer focus.

### Cards & Narrative Portals
- Backed by nocturnal glass (`rgba(22, 9, 15, 0.65)`) with `28px` backdrop-filter blur.
- Framed with an asymmetric top-lit 1px gradient hairline: transitioning from `rgba(255, 181, 200, 0.35)` at the top edge to `rgba(217, 180, 119, 0.05)` at the base.
- Internal padding utilizes `space-md` on mobile and `space-lg` on desktop.

### Chips & Metadata Emblems
- Compact capsules built on deep wine translucency (`rgba(59, 16, 36, 0.4)`).
- Typography strictly configured to `label-caps` in Champagne Gold (`#D9B477`).
- Active selection introduces a soft, pulsing starlight beacon (2px illuminated blush pin-dot).

### Input Fields & Curated Selectors
- Flat obsidian glass base (`rgba(14, 5, 10, 0.8)`) with recessed 1px borders in smoked mauve (`#644E57`).
- Active and focus states ignite the hairline into Romantic Crimson (`#E83F67`) accompanied by a subtle inward ambient glow.
- Cursor and placeholder render in warm ash rose (`#B89F9E`).

### Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios
- Bespoke diamond or micro-circular geometry; default state shows a refined hairline contour in Champagne Gold.
- Checked state reveals an illuminated crimson core surrounded by a celestial starlight aura. No utilitarian checkmark glyphs; selection is confirmed via geometric illumination.

### Lists & Navigation Rails
- Fluid vertical list designs separated by hairline horizontal rules (`rgba(217, 180, 119, 0.1)`).
- Hovering over a list item engages an ambient midnight burgundy flood layer (`#2A0A19`) with an effortless left-border blush illumination.
